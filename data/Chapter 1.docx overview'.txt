Plot Overview
The Echo of Kings follows the journey of Leo, a young warrior who rises from humble beginnings to become a legendary hero. With his immense power and unwavering ideals, Leo seeks to protect the world from an ancient evil force known as The Echo—a destructive, otherworldly energy that can consume anyone who dares to wield it. Over the course of the novel, Leo grapples with internal and external challenges, his own humanity, the cost of leadership, and the weight of his destiny. His story is one of growth, sacrifice, and the eventual passing of the torch to a new hero.

1.	The Call to Adventure:
o	Introduction to Leo: Leo is introduced as a young, skilled fighter with dreams of becoming a hero, despite his humble origins. His village is on the brink of war with a neighboring kingdom. Leo is not the strongest or the most skilled but has an innate, natural talent for combat and a strong sense of justice.
o	The Prophecy of the Echo: The Echo is introduced as a mysterious and powerful force that has plagued the world for centuries. It can bestow immense power upon a chosen few but often corrupts them, leading to madness or death.
o	Leo’s First Battle: Leo is thrust into a large-scale conflict where he showcases his leadership abilities and begins to draw attention. After the battle, Leo encounters a strange force—The Echo—after defeating an enemy leader who was seemingly consumed by it.

o	Leo's First Confrontation: Leo faces off against one of the Forsaken, a former king who was overtaken by the Echo and now leads a growing army.
o	The Echo’s Temptation: Leo faces temptation when the Echo promises him unmatched power to defeat his enemies. The struggle within him intensifies as he tries to balance his humanity with the lure of power.

o	Civil Strife: Under Leo’s leadership, factions begin to fragment, some fearing the influence of the Echo, while others rally behind it. His once-united kingdom begins to splinter into civil war.
o	The Final Betrayal: Leo is betrayed by someone he trusted—a former ally who becomes consumed by the Echo and challenges Leo for control. In the resulting confrontation, Leo is severely wounded, both physically and emotionally.

o	Leo’s Descent: At the lowest point of the story, Leo is alone, weakened, and on the brink of losing everything. His connection to the Echo becomes so strong that it begins to tear at the fabric of his soul, threatening to consume him completely.
o	The Choice: Leo faces a choice—he can give in to the Echo and gain ultimate power, but at the cost of his humanity, or he can sacrifice everything and let the Echo go.
